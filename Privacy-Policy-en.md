# iFrameMaster Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 10, 2026**  
**Last Updated: November 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how iFrameMaster (the “App”) handles and protects information. The App is provided by **LIHUALI** (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

## 1. Our Privacy Approach

The App is designed primarily for on-device processing. We do not operate servers that receive user data from the App, and the App does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

## 2. Information Processed by the App

### 2.1 Images and Files You Select

When you choose screenshots, background images, or sticker images, the App reads the selected files so that it can create and edit preview images on your Mac.

- Images are processed on your device;
- The App does not automatically upload images to us or to servers operated by us;
- The App does not scan images that you have not selected;
- Imported images are not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or model training;
- Unless you choose to export, the App does not independently write edited results to folders other than a location you select.

When exporting, you choose the destination folder. The App writes generated images only to the selected destination. You control and may delete exported files at any time.

### 2.2 Local Preferences

The App stores a limited number of preferences on your device, such as interface language and theme color. These settings are stored in the App’s local container or system-provided preferences storage and are not transmitted to us by the App.

### 2.3 App Store Purchase Information

The App uses Apple StoreKit to provide a one-time purchase and purchase-restoration functionality. Apple processes purchases and payments. We do not receive or store your payment card number, complete Apple Account details, or payment credentials through the App.

To determine whether Pro features are unlocked, the App reads the product identifier, transaction verification result, and current entitlement status provided by Apple on your device. The current version does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Apple’s handling of related information is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy and terms. For more information, see the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

The current version does not actively collect or transmit the following information to us through the App:

- Your name, telephone number, email address, or postal address;
- Precise or approximate location;
- Contacts, calendars, microphone recordings, or camera data;
- Browsing history, search history, or advertising identifiers;
- Information used to track you across apps or websites;
- The contents of images you import, edit, or export;
- Usage analytics or advertising data directly collected by the App.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

Because the current version does not transmit the user content or personal information described above to us, we do not sell, rent, or share that information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

Apple may process related information as an independent provider of App Store distribution, StoreKit purchases, and operating-system services. Apple’s processing is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

If we are legally required to disclose information actually under our control, we will do so only to the extent required by applicable law. The current version does not operate developer servers that store user content from the App.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

- Imported images are used for the current app session and editing operation. The App does not retain copies on a developer-operated server after you close or reset the relevant editing session;
- Preferences such as language and theme are stored on your Mac and can be removed by changing app settings, clearing app data, or uninstalling the App;
- Exported images remain in the folder you selected and may be deleted through Finder at any time;
- App Store purchase records are managed by Apple and are subject to Apple’s retention and deletion practices.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or user-content servers, there are no images, projects, or user profiles stored in a remote account that we can delete on your behalf. You may contact us using the information below if you have questions about information handled by the App.

## 6. Data Security

The App uses macOS features such as App Sandbox, code signing, and system file-selection mechanisms to limit file access. No software can guarantee absolute security, but we make reasonable efforts to maintain least-privilege access and to address privacy or security issues that come to our attention.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed primarily to children and does not require users to create an account or submit their age, name, or contact details. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe the App’s actual behavior is inconsistent with this Policy, please contact us.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

- Avoid selecting images, in which case the App will not read them;
- Cancel a file-selection or export operation;
- Delete exported files;
- Change or clear locally stored preferences;
- Manage App Store purchases using Apple’s account and purchase-management features;
- Contact us with an inquiry, objection, or other request available under applicable law.

Because the current version does not create developer user accounts or upload user content to developer-operated servers, we generally do not hold a remote user profile that can be identified or exported.

## 9. International Data Transfers

The current version does not transmit user-selected images, editing projects, or local preferences to developer-operated servers. Apple may process and transfer related information when providing the App Store and StoreKit services in accordance with Apple’s privacy policy.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s functionality, data-handling practices, or applicable requirements change. The revised Policy will show a new “Last Updated” date. For material changes, we will provide notice in an appropriate manner and obtain consent where required by applicable law.
